The idea of the project was to show how much of what we have in a typical Polish house comes from China.

Pitfall #1: as it turns out,production and trade data speak different “classification languages” in the European Union. Prodcom groups by what a product is made of, while HS codes group imports by what the product is used for. So the codes don’t line up!!

Pitfall #2:AI is (relatively) good at removing things but not at adding them. So I had to plan the end result first and then remove objects from it.

The house illustration came together in two stages: generating the first draft, then making little tweaks. Each stage needed a different AI tool.

Stage 1: the first draft

The prompt was a long list of items to include in every room and a couple of design examples.
