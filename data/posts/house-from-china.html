The house illustration came together in two stages: generating the first draft, then making little tweaks. Each stage needed a different AI tool.

Stage 1: the first draft

The prompt: “Create an image of a house in the highest possible resolution, with all the items from my list and all the rooms. The image should be on a transparent background. If something you’d find in a regular house is missing, you can draw it in too. Style: light colours, not very saturated. It should look like a draft drawn in design software.”

Stage 2: little tweaks

Nano Banana’s problem is that it can’t add or remove a single thing. So I took the image to ChatGPT, which is good at small changes. Here’s what both tools did with the prompt “Remove the white pillar in the wall in the bathroom”:

But there was a catch: Whisk exports images at 2.5K pixels, while ChatGPT returns 1,600 at most. So I took ChatGPT’s version to Adobe Firefly and upscaled it to 4K.
